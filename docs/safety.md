# Safety

AAW Lab sEMG modules and examples are for education, research prototyping, electronics development, and non-diagnostic experiments.

They are not medical devices.

## Do Not Use For

- Medical diagnosis.
- Treatment or therapy.
- Vital-sign monitoring.
- Safety-critical control.
- Emergency or life-support systems.

## Power Safety

- Use battery power for first human-connected tests.
- Do not connect a human-connected setup to mains-referenced instruments.
- Be careful with USB-powered laptops, desktop PCs, oscilloscopes, bench supplies, and chargers.
- If you are unsure whether a setup is isolated, do not connect it to a person.

## Electrode Safety

- Use clean electrodes and clean skin.
- Do not place electrodes on broken, irritated, or injured skin.
- Do not place electrodes across the chest.
- Stop the test if there is discomfort, skin irritation, heat, pain, or unexpected behavior.

## Development Notes

- Start with low-risk forearm experiments.
- Keep wiring short and strain-relieved.
- Avoid loose breadboard power connections for human-connected tests.
- Verify the circuit output with a simulator or signal source before testing on skin.
